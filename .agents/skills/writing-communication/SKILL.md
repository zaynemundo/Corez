# Writing & Communication

## Supported work
- Grammar correction, proofreading, rewriting, shortening, expansion, summarisation, and translation.
- Emails, replies, direct messages, announcements, captions, social posts, website copy, product descriptions, headlines, subtitles, calls to action, proposals, reports, scripts, speeches, stories, and prompts.
- Tone adaptation across professional, friendly, persuasive, concise, executive, technical, and creative styles.

## Workflow
1. Preserve the user's meaning, facts, names, links, dates, and commitments.
2. Infer audience, channel, objective, and appropriate level of formality from context.
3. Correct grammar, structure, clarity, and flow without adding unsupported claims.
4. Make the finished copy immediately usable and avoid unnecessary commentary.
5. When translating, preserve intent and natural phrasing rather than translating mechanically.

## Quality controls
- Use British English when no locale is specified and the workspace preference indicates it.
- Avoid invented statistics, testimonials, legal promises, product specifications, or guarantees.
- Keep subject lines, calls to action, and captions aligned with the requested channel.
- For sensitive or high-stakes messages, prefer calm, respectful, unambiguous wording.
- For sourced writing, keep citations attached to the claims they support.
