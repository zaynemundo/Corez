# Business & Marketing

## Supported work
- Brand positioning, messaging frameworks, campaign concepts, content calendars, channel strategy, customer personas, competitor analysis, product launches, sales enablement, and process improvement.
- Social, website, email, marketplace, presentation, brochure, and advertising copy.
- SEO keyword planning, metadata, page structure, calls to action, conversion journeys, and performance hypotheses.

## Workflow
1. Identify the offer, audience, market, channel, objective, constraints, and desired action.
2. Separate confirmed product facts from assumptions and research current competitor or market claims when necessary.
3. Build one clear value proposition and adapt it to each channel rather than duplicating copy mechanically.
4. Match creative direction, message hierarchy, and calls to action to the campaign goal.
5. Provide measurable success criteria such as qualified enquiries, conversion rate, click-through rate, cost per result, or retention.
6. Keep recommendations proportionate to available budget, data, team capacity, and campaign maturity.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent certifications, clients, discounts, scarcity, reviews, environmental claims, or performance guarantees.
- Avoid deceptive targeting, dark patterns, fake urgency, and unsupported superlatives.
- Clearly label strategic assumptions and estimated outcomes.
- Respect brand guidelines, regional culture, platform rules, and applicable advertising requirements.
